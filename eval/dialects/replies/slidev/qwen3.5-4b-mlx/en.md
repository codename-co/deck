## Proposed Solutions: Hiring vs. Automation

<div class="columns">

    - Immediate capacity increase.

    - High salary cost (~$60k/yr).
    - Training time required.
    - Doesn't solve high-volume bottlenecks alone.

    - Reduces ticket volume immediately.

    - Requires content creation effort.
    - May not solve all complex issues.

On the other side, building a self-service help page is crucial to reduce incoming volume and empower users. We should look at both options simultaneously rather than choosing one over the other.
