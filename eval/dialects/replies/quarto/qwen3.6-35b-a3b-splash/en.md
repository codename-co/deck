## The Challenge

::: {.notes}
This section highlights the current pressure points in our support operations. We're seeing consistent growth in both volume and complexity, which is directly impacting our response capabilities and team bandwidth.
:::

---

# Support Metrics: July–September

- **Ticket Volume:** 420 (Jul) → 510 (Aug) → 610 (Sep)
- **Avg First Response Time:** 4.5 hrs → 9 hrs

::: {.notes}
Tickets grew by ~45% over three months. Response times doubled, indicating clear capacity strain. Without intervention, we risk customer satisfaction drops and team burnout.
:::

---

## Proposed Solutions

::: {.columns}
:: {.column}
### 🧑‍💻 Hire a Second Support Person
- Directly reduces ticket backlog
- Cuts response times in half
- Improves team morale & coverage

:: {.column}
### 📖 Build a Self-Service Help Page
- Answers 30–40% of common queries
- Reduces repeat tickets
- Scales without additional headcount
:::

::: {.notes}
Both options address different aspects of the problem. Staffing handles volume directly, while self-service reduces repeat inquiries and empowers users. We recommend pursuing both for maximum impact.
:::

---

- Phase 2: Draft & Publish Self-Service Content (Week 3–4) { .fragment }

- Phase 4: Launch, Train, & Monitor Feedback (Week 7–8) { .fragment }

::: {.notes}
This phased approach ensures we mitigate response times quickly while building long-term scalability. Each phase has clear deliverables and success metrics.
