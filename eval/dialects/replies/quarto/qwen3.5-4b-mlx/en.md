# Support Team Status & Proposal

**Ticket Volume Trends & Response Time Analysis**  
**Proposed Solutions for Q4 Growth**

## Speaker Notes
Welcome everyone. Today is to discuss the current state of our support team based on Q3 data and propose a strategic plan for Q4. We have seen significant growth in ticket volume, which has impacted our response times. I want to walk you through the data and propose a dual approach: hiring additional staff and implementing self-service tools.

---

## Ticket Volume & Response Time Trends

| Metric | July | August | September |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Support Tickets** | 420 | 510 | 610 |
| **Avg. Response Time** | 4.5 hrs | 9.0 hrs | 9.0 hrs |

*   **Ticket Volume:** Increased by ~45% from July to September.
*   **Response Time:** Dropped significantly, doubling from 4.5 hours to 9 hours.
*   **Trend:** Linear growth in volume correlates with degradation in SLA performance.

## Speaker Notes
Here is the raw data from our helpdesk system over the last three months. July started at 420 tickets with a healthy 4.5-hour response time. By August, we hit 510 tickets and the average jumped to 9 hours. September saw a further jump to 610 tickets with no improvement in response time. This indicates our current capacity is being overwhelmed, and we are missing SLA targets.

## Proposed Solutions: Hiring vs. Self-Service

<div style="display: flex; gap: 2rem;">
    <div class="column">
        <h3>1. Hire Second Support Person</h3>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Immediate Impact:</strong> Reduces ticket backlog.</li>
            <li><strong>Cost:</strong> $45k-$60k annual salary + benefits.</li>
            <li><strong>Focus:</strong> Complex tickets & escalations.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="column">
        <h3>2. Build Self-Service Help Page</h3>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Long-term Efficiency:</strong> Deflects common queries.</li>
            <li><strong>Cost:</strong> Low (Content creation).</li>
            <li><strong>Focus:</strong> Tier 1 FAQs & documentation.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
</div>

## Speaker Notes
We are proposing a two-pronged strategy. First, hiring a second support person provides immediate relief to handle the volume spike and maintain response times. Second, building a self-service help page is cost-effective for long-term efficiency by reducing repetitive Tier 1 tickets. Combining these offers the best ROI.

## Speaker Notes
Let's review why we need to act now. First, the volume is unsustainable; one person cannot handle 610 tickets effectively. Second, our response time degradation risks customer churn as users become frustrated with slow support. Third, self-service tools reduce long-term costs by deflecting queries. Finally, the combined approach ensures stability for our current growth and sets us up for future expansion.
